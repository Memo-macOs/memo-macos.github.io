Memo for Mac

A simple app for quickly jotting down notes on your Mac. Pop open a new memo in less than a second—perfect for phone numbers, messages, meeting notes, or fleeting ideas.

Visual Organization

Assign colors to memos for instant recognition: yellow, red, green, blue, or purple. Rich text support with dozens of font families keeps everything clear and styled just right.

Privacy First

On shared or public Macs, lock individual memos with a password. Content stays visible on-screen but hidden until unlocked. Use Touch ID for even faster access.

Seamless Sync

iCloud keeps your memos in sync across all your Macs. Set auto-lock intervals to ensure privacy without constant manual effort.

Effortless Workflow

Touch Bar integration brings memo controls right to your fingertips. Launch, write, and manage without disrupting your flow.


How It Works

1. Launch Instantly

Hit a global shortcut to open a new memo in under a second—no menus, no hassle.

2. Write and Color

Type with rich text, pick a color, and format as needed. Everything stays on top until you're done.

3. Secure and Sync

Lock with password or Touch ID, set auto-lock, and let iCloud sync across devices.

4. Stay Organized

Manage memos visually by color, resize freely, and keep your desktop tidy.


FAQ

Do I need to buy anything?

No—this is the fully activated version of Memo 1.8. Everything works out of the box.

How do I launch a new memo?

Use the global shortcut (customizable in settings) for instant access from anywhere.

Can I sync across devices?

Yes, iCloud sync keeps memos updated across all your Macs automatically.

What about privacy on shared Macs?

Password-protect individual memos or use Touch ID. Auto-lock adds extra security.

Supported on which macOS versions?

Optimized for the latest macOS, with Touch Bar and Touch ID support where available.
